What I did

I compared three OCR engines and two transcription engines, with reviewed licenses and pseudonymization. PaddleOCR's CER is 0.22 on photos, 0.48 on printed text and 0.81 on manuscripts.

It works for printed text and studio speech. Colonial manuscripts and street interviews still need human correction.

Hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud: if it has been idle, it takes a moment to wake up.
