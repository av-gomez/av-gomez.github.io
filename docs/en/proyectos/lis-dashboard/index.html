What I did

As a research assistant to Dr. Isabel Guerrero (Department of Economics, PUCP), I checked the availability and quality of each variable in the Luxembourg Income Study, for example hi31 non-contributory pension, hc6 health spending and dhi disposable income.

The site has five views: suggested combinations, availability matrix, free cross-tab, country sheet and dictionary. For any combination of variables, it shows in which countries and years they are available at the same time.

LIS microdata are not redistributed. The site only shows availability metadata.
