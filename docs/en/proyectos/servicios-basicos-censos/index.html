What I did

Final group project for R Fundamentals (Q-LAB PUCP diploma), with Oscar Chávez, Fabricio Luna and Karen Macedo. Cleaning of the 2007 and 2017 National Censuses, district indicators and a reproducible Quarto report. Nationally, access to water rose from 63.7 % to 78.6 %.
