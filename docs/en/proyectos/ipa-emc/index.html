What I did

IPA evaluates this World Bank funded program with an experimental design. It has an AI math tutor and an AI vocational tutor.

What I do: I fixed the progress report dashboard, follow up on the scheduling tracker and prepared the enumerator training presentation in Overleaf. Before that I organized the audio files of the qualitative component.

Ongoing evaluation: only the general design and my tasks appear here, with no results or data.
