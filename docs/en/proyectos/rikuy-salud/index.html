What I did

On the RENIPRESS registry (cutoff: September 30, 2026) we proposed an MVP focused on a single function: guide and route. We developed it in the 2026 Entrepreneurship Lab of EQUIPU PUCP, a program with more than 80 institutions.

We left out appointment booking (there are no public APIs) and personal health records (they require complying with Law 29733 on personal data). Hybrid model: free for users, income from agreements with the State.
