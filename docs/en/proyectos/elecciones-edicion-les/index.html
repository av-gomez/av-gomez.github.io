What I did

I directly supported the comparison of government plans for the 2026 elections. The traffic light shows whether a proposal is in the plan and explained, is in the plan but not explained, or is not found. I also supported the Propuestas en verde guide and the Nakama y nación panel.

As academic research coordinator I check that texts are well written, have sound academic content and can go through other experts. Some columns I reviewed: GDP and social well-being, vouchers and public education and polarization and human rights.
