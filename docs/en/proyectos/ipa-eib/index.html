What I did

Comparative study funded by the IDB. The self-administered survey asks about training, language command, relationship with the community, practices of the bilingual model, materials and intention to keep teaching.

What I do: I update the Peru and Suriname report (download from SurveyCTO, cleaning and reports in Stata) and Peru's progress dashboard in R Shiny.

Ongoing study: only the general design and my tasks appear here, with no results or data.
