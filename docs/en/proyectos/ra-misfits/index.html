What I did

Research assistance on the study Rules-in-Use as Evidence of Institutional Fit, by Cathy Rubiños and Isabel Guerrero, on why non-compliance persists among small forest enterprises seeking to formalize.

It is a qualitative case study based on field interviews. Formal rules and rules-in-use are coded with Institutional Grammar.

Ongoing study: its results are not shown here. The figures describe the study area.
