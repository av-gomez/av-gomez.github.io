What I did

I cleaned the MINSA IPRESS registry, merged it with the 2017 Census and published a dashboard with 6 indicators and 6 visualizations that recalculate with the filters. The correlation between facility density and level II and III hospitals per capita is 0.15.

I also made a 54-second reel narrated with my AI-cloned voice, with a visible notice throughout the video.
